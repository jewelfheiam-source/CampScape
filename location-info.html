Getting to Oriental Mindoro

Oriental Mindoro is reached from Luzon almost entirely by sea through Batangas Port, about 2 to 3 hours south of Manila by bus. Three main gateways serve campers:

GatewayCrossingTravel TimeServesNotes
Puerto Galera (Balatero, Muelle, Sabang piers)Batangas to Puerto Galera fastcraft / RoRo45 min to 1 hr 15 minWhite Beach, Sabang, Tamaraw Falls, Tukuran Falls, Mt. HalconDaytime sailings, roughly 7 daily; weekend extras
Calapan City PortBatangas to Calapan RoRo and fastcraftabout 1 to 2 hoursCalapan, Naujan Lake, Baco, eastern townsSailings nearly every hour, 24/7; busiest gateway
Roxas PortBatangas to Roxas RoRo (select days)3 to 4 hoursSouthern towns: Bulalacao islands, MansalayCheck sailing days; otherwise route via Calapan then bus south

Fares and schedules change seasonally; verify with operators (Montenegro, FastCat, SuperCat, OceanJet, Starlite) before travel. See Puerto Galera travel directions and Starlite route information. Published one-way passenger fares ranged from about ₱250 (economy RoRo) to ₱770 (business/fastcraft) at the time of writing.

Bulalacao and the southern islands

Bulalacao is the southernmost municipality, jump-off for the Suguicay, Aslom, and Buyayao island group.

Gateway Towns: Stay, Eat, Stock Up

Calapan City

The provincial capital and main transport hub: the busiest port, supermarkets, banks and ATMs, hospitals, and dining from carinderias to restaurants like Arsenia's Hapag Kainan sa Kabukiran (per the provincial tourism guide). Stock up here before heading to quieter campsites.

Puerto Galera

Complete tourist infrastructure: dive shops, restaurants (including Luca Cucina Italiana per the provincial guide), guesthouses, ATMs, and ferry connections. Base yourself here for falls trips and dive weekends.

Bulalacao

Small-town charm with resorts such as Balatasan Beach Resort and eateries like Cafe Francesca per the provincial tourism guide. Last full stop for supplies before island camping; signal is limited on the islands.
