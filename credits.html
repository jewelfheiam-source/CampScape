Academic Disclaimer

CampScape is a student academic project for Applied Business Tools and Technologies in Tourism. It is not a real tour operator. All contact details, prices marked estimated, itineraries, testimonials, community posts, and promo codes are sample content for demonstration. Destination facts were researched from the official provincial tourism website (travelorientalmindoro.ph), municipal tourism pages, and published transport schedules, and are accurate to the best of the author's knowledge at the time of writing.
