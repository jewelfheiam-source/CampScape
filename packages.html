Tour Packages & Itineraries

Three sample adventures, ready to customize. Every price below is an estimate for planning and is confirmed only after we check with local providers.

Important: These are sample packages with estimated prices and unconfirmed itineraries, prepared for an academic project. They demonstrate how CampScape would sell tour packages; actual bookings are subject to confirmation with campsite owners and local tourism providers.

Nature Escape Package

3 days, 2 nights · Calapan City, Naujan & Baco · Best for families and first-time campers

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 - Arrival and lakeside settle-in

Meet at Calapan City, van to Naujan. Pitch tents by Lake Naujan, afternoon birdwatching and paddle, welcome dinner over the camp stove.

Day 2 - Forest and falls

Morning lakeside sunrise, drive to Baco river country for an easy forest walk and swim, picnic lunch, return to camp for campfire games and stargazing.

Day 3 - Calapan City and farewell

Breakfast, pack-down with Leave No Trace sweep, short Calapan City heritage and pasalubong stop, transfer to Calapan Port by noon.

Inclusions

  • 2 nights tent camping (tents provided, twin sharing)
  • 6 meals (2 breakfasts, 2 lunches, 2 dinners)
  • Round-trip van transfers within Mindoro
  • Local guide fees and park registration (estimated)
  • Camp cooking gear and first-aid kit

Exclusions

  • Ferry fare from Batangas to Calapan (approx. ₱250 to ₱770 one way depending on class and operator)
  • Personal camping gear and sleeping bags
  • Snacks, souvenirs, and optional activities

Estimated ₱4,500 per person (based on 10 pax)

Availability: year-round; best from December to May. Book at least 7 days ahead.

Beach Camping Adventure

3 days, 2 nights · Puerto Galera · Best for barkadas, couples, and weekenders

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 - Ferry, beach, and bonfire

Meet at Batangas Port, morning fastcraft to Puerto Galera, check in at the beachfront camp on White Beach, free-time swimming, sunset viewing, and a beachfront bonfire night.

Day 2 - Island hop and falls

Island-hopping and snorkeling at the coral gardens (swimming where permitted), afternoon trip to Tamaraw Falls, evening free along the beach strip.

Day 3 - Sunrise and sail home

Sunrise beach walk, breakfast, free morning, afternoon ferry back to Batangas Port.

Inclusions

  • 2 nights beachfront tent camping (tents and mats provided)
  • Round-trip Batangas to Puerto Galera ferry assistance (fare excluded)
  • Island-hopping boat rental (shared)
  • Snorkeling gear rental
  • Tamaraw Falls side trip and entrance (estimated)
  • Local coordinator and first-aid kit

Exclusions

  • Ferry fare (approx. ₱300 to ₱600 one way depending on operator and class)
  • Meals not listed, drinks, and personal expenses

Estimated ₱5,800 per person (based on 8 pax)

Availability: best March to June; weekends fill fast. Book at least 10 days ahead.

Adventure & Exploration Package

4 days, 3 nights · Bulalacao island circuit · Best for thrill-seekers and photo hunters

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 - Southbound

Assemble at Roxas Port (or Calapan), van south to Bulalacao, boat to Suguicay Island, set up beach camp, sunset swim and orientation.

Day 2 - Suguicay to Aslom

Morning snorkeling over seagrass and reef flats, island-hop to Aslom Island, beach camping with a castaway-movie bonfire night.

Day 3 - Buyayao and Balatasan

Day circuit to Buyayao Island and Balatasan Long Beach (free entry), floating cottages, beach games, and a final island dinner.

Day 4 - Return north

Pack-down and cleanup, boat back to Bulalacao proper, van to Roxas or Calapan Port for the afternoon ferry.

Inclusions

  • 3 nights island beach camping (tents provided)
  • All island-hopping boat transfers within Bulalacao
  • 7 meals plus drinking water refills
  • Local boatman and guide fees (estimated)
  • Snorkeling masks and fins (shared sets)
  • Camping, cooking, and safety equipment

Exclusions

  • Long-distance transport to Mindoro and between ports
  • Island entrance and environmental fees (paid on site, amounts to be confirmed)
  • Personal gear and travel insurance

Estimated ₱7,200 per person (based on 6 pax)

Availability: February to October, weather permitting. Book at least 14 days ahead.
