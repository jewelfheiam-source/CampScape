Featured Destination

Puerto Galera: A Bay of World-Class Beauty

Puerto Galera, the gateway of Oriental Mindoro, was voted into the Club of the Most Beautiful Bays in the World in 2004. Its protected natural harbor shelters divers, swimmers, and campers alike, and White Beach and Sabang are famous for sunsets, snorkeling, and nightlife.

  • Beachfront camping and resort-style tent stays at White Beach and Talipanan
  • Dive sites with rich coral reefs and frequent turtle sightings
  • Side trips to Tamaraw Falls and Tukuran Falls and lagoon
  • Only 3 to 4 hours from Manila by bus and ferry
