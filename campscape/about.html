About CampScape

A camping tourism platform built to put Oriental Mindoro's great outdoors on every traveler's map.

Our Story

Born Around a Campfire

CampScape began as a student project with a simple observation: Oriental Mindoro has some of the most beautiful camping country in the Philippines, from the dive bays of Puerto Galera to the still-quiet islands of Bulalacao, yet travelers rarely find one honest place to plan a camping trip here.

CampScape answers that gap. It is a tourism platform dedicated to promoting camping destinations and outdoor adventures across the province, connecting campers directly with campsite owners, boatmen, guides, and tourism offices, and making it easy to plan, compare, and book outdoor experiences.

This website is an academic project for Applied Business Tools and Technologies in Tourism. Contact details, prices, and reviews shown as samples are clearly labeled.

Our Mission

To make camping in Oriental Mindoro easy, safe, and rewarding by providing accurate destination information, transparent estimated costs, and direct booking and inquiry channels that support local tourism businesses.

Our Vision

To be the trusted online home of camping tourism in Oriental Mindoro, where every traveler discovers the province's natural wonders and every community benefits from responsible visitors.

Why It Matters

Tourism Significance and Competitive Advantages

Camping-First Focus

Unlike general travel sites, CampScape is built entirely around camping: tent sites, facilities, fees, weather, and gear, so campers get answers generic portals miss.

Local First

We direct inquiries straight to campsite owners, local boat operators, and municipal tourism offices, keeping tourism income within Mindoro communities.

Beyond the Beaten Path

While everyone knows White Beach, we actively promote lesser-known gems such as Balatasan Long Beach, Lake Naujan, and the Bulalacao island group.

Honest Estimates

Fees, fares, and package prices are labeled as verified or estimated, so travelers can budget with confidence and no false promises.

Responsible Travel

Every guide includes Leave No Trace practices, campsite rules, and wildlife protection reminders to keep destinations beautiful for the next generation.

Community Powered

Our blog and community section let campers share real experiences, tips, and photos, turning every trip into advice for the next adventurer.

Our Market

Who CampScape Serves

  • Local and international tourists seeking beaches, mountains, and islands
  • Families and friend groups planning safe, budget-friendly weekends
  • Students and backpackers chasing affordable adventure
  • Nature lovers and camping enthusiasts who value quiet, uncrowded sites
  • Local campsite owners and tourism businesses looking to reach more guests
